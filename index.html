Verify Your Widows' & Orphans' Pension Eligibility

A fast, secure, and automated way for public officers or their beneficiaries to determine their eligibility for the W&OP scheme. Ensure your details are ready before proceeding to the verification tool.

Launch Eligibility Checker View Instructions

Before You Begin

What you need to know before accessing the tool.

1. Have Your Details Ready

You must be ready with personal identification, employment, and dependent details, along with certified supporting documents. This may includes the NIC, birth certificate, death certificate, appointment letter, salary details, marriage certificate, spouse and children’s information, and institutional certification. These information will be used to calculate your eligibility status based on current W&OP regulations.

2. Instant Verification

The system will instantly calculate your eligibility status based on current W&OP regulations without requiring a manual visit to the department.

3. Next Steps

If eligible, you will be guided on how to proceed with formal registration and which specific departmental forms you need to submit.
